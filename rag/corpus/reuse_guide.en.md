# Makerspace reuse guide (English summary)

## Reuse-first hierarchy
Waste hierarchy under German and EU law: prevent → reuse → recycle → recover → dispose. In a makerspace this means: catalogue offcuts and parts before they reach a bin, publish an inventory, and let the next project pick from it.

## Quick reference by material
- Steel, aluminium, copper: reuse unlimited; otherwise scrap metal at the Wertstoffhof.
- PLA: not home-compostable; residual waste or in-house filament recycler.
- PETG/ABS parts: not packaging; residual waste or in-house recycler.
- Plywood/MDF: waste-wood category A II; recycling centre, never burned indoors.
- PCBs, motors, cables: e-waste (WEEE/ElektroG), free take-back.
- Lithium cells: hazardous; tape terminals, battery collection box only.
- Cardboard: paper bin; acrylic: residual waste or recycling centre, not the glass container.

## Condition assessment
Good: no visible damage, threads clean, no delamination. Worn: cosmetic wear, still functional. Damaged: cracks, corrosion on load paths, bulging cells – route to disposal, never to reuse.
