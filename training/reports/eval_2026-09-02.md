## Known weaknesses (hand-written after inspecting `runs/eval/confusion_matrix.png`)
- The dominant failure is **missed detection**, not confusion: true→background counts are nut_bolt 424 (vs 132 found),
  glass 197, cardboard 146, plastic_container 61, motor 35, tool 33, battery 24. Cross-class confusion is single-digit
  (glass↔plastic_container 7/4, cardboard↔plastic 2/3). Cause: small, distant instances in LVIS/TACO scenes at imgsz 640.
- `screw` (AP50 0.995) and `pcb` (0.994) are inflated: their test images are single-object studio shots (see
  `../dataset/SOURCES.md`). Treat them as "recognises a screw/PCB filling the frame", not clutter performance.
- `battery` (0.030) and `tool` (0.049) have too little real data (26 / 35 test boxes) to be usable yet.
- Trained on **public data only** (v0-public); the six makerspace-only classes have no data and are never predicted.
  Next data step: own bench photos, especially battery/tool close-ups and the six empty classes.
